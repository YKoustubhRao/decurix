Rules
Case doesn't matter. Leave one empty to match on the other alone; with both, both must match. The sender pattern is tried on the address (billing@acme.com) and on the whole From line (Acme Billing <billing@acme.com>).
{subject}, {sender}, {from} and {date} are filled in. The email itself is written out below the request, and the request repeated after it - except for a request that starts with / (like /skill name value="{subject}"), which is sent exactly as written.
