Use this phone’s apps in your VMs?

Your desktops can then open the apps installed on {{.Name}} and use them for you. The apps run on this phone, on a screen of their own, so they work while it is in your pocket.

Android only allows this through its developer tools: you install one free app and turn on one switch. Decurix does the rest.

⚠ Read this before you say yes

Linking gives your Decurix full control of this phone, the same as a developer’s cable:

  • Any app can be opened and used - banking, payments, messages, email, photos - by you and by the AI agents in your desktops.
  • The agents’ “ask before paying or sending” check cannot see inside phone apps. An agent could tap Send or Pay in one without stopping to ask you.
  • Everything the apps show - messages, balances, one-time codes - can be seen on your desktops.
  • Anyone who can use your Decurix dashboard, or holds this phone’s pairing, gets the same control. Link only a Decurix you own and trust.
  • Wireless debugging stays on, and the phone stays awake while linked, which uses more battery.

To undo it at any time: Revoke this phone in the Decurix Mobile menu, turn off Wireless debugging in Developer options, and uninstall Termux.
