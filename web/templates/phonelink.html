Use this phone’s apps in your VMs?

Your desktops can then open the apps installed on {{.Name}} and use them for you. The apps run on this phone, on a screen of their own, so they work while it is in your pocket.

Android only allows this through its developer tools: you install one free app and turn on one switch. Decurix does the rest.

⚠ What linking allows

Linking lets your Decurix open and use any app on this phone, the same as a developer’s cable. That covers banking, payments, messages and email, and it applies to you and to the AI agents in your desktops.

  • Agents act only on a task you give them. Every agent is told to stop and ask you before it pays, buys, sends a message for you, or does anything else with real-world consequences.
  • Send, Pay and Buy are blocked until you approve them. The VM reads the app’s buttons and fields from Android itself, the same way a screen reader does. An agent’s tap on a Send or Pay button, or pressing Enter in a messaging app, waits for your yes, just as it does in a desktop’s browser.
  • A few cases can’t be read, and only the agent’s instruction to ask you covers them: apps that draw their own screen without labels (some games and custom-built apps), and phones older than Android 11. The app’s window in the VM says “Picture only” when this is the case.
  • Your desktops can see whatever these apps show, including messages, balances and one-time codes. So can anyone who can use your Decurix dashboard or has this phone’s pairing. Only link a Decurix you trust.
  • Wireless debugging stays on and the phone stays awake while it is linked, which uses more battery.

To undo it at any time: revoke this phone in the Decurix Mobile menu, turn off Wireless debugging in Developer options, and uninstall Termux.
