What we're building

Software gave you tools.
Decurix gives you workers.

A Decurix worker is an AI with a computer of its own: a full desktop with a browser, spreadsheets, office apps and your business software. It takes a task off your plate the way a capable new hire would. It does the work, asks you about what it can't decide alone, and remembers what you told it.

Its own computer

Every worker gets a private desktop that runs on your PC. It uses the apps your team already uses: Chrome, Excel or LibreOffice, Word, PowerPoint, your ERP, government and bank portals, the inbox. There's nothing to integrate first.

You stay in charge

When something needs your judgement, it raises a query instead of guessing. Anything that sends, pays or publishes waits for your yes, and one yes covers one action. Emails go to an Outbox as drafts for you to send.

It learns on the job

Your answers and corrections become its memory: lessons, rules and skills it reads before every task. Work it has proven turns into its own scripts. Over time you're needed less and each task costs less.

How it works

Brief it like a colleague.
Check its work like a manager.

  1. 1

    Create a worker

    Click New desktop, choose its brain (Claude, GPT, or a model that runs on your own PC) and, if you like, share one folder from your computer.

  2. 2

    Give it work in chat

    One conversation per worker, on your laptop or your phone. Attach the files and say what you need, in your own words.

  3. 3

    It works on its own screen

    It opens apps, reads files, fills forms and writes documents. Watch it live, or don't. Every action is recorded with before-and-after screenshots.

  4. 4

    It asks when it should

    Questions and approvals land in one place, Needs you. Answer in a word. The answer is remembered, and it never asks the same thing twice.

  5. 5

    Done means checked

    Totals, files, paths and recipients are checked in code before any model's opinion. Only a confirmed result is reported done, and you can mark any answer Correct or Wrong.

  6. 6

    It gets better

    Every task is on the record: what was asked, what it did, where you stepped in. You can also record yourself doing a job. It studies the recording and asks about anything it didn't follow.

Principles

Correctness > intelligence
>> efficiency.

In that order. A worker may ask, or say it failed. It must never report success for something it did not see.

No unseen success

Facts a program can decide (sums, files, paths, recipients) are checked in code before any model gets a vote.

A yes is one action's

Send, pay and publish stop for approval every time. There's no "always allow", and no skill can switch the gate off.

Autonomy is earned

A skill stays an apprentice until three clean, checked runs in a row. One slip and it's learning again.

The model is swappable

A smaller model with your answers should reach the same result as a bigger one alone. A smarter model just asks you less.

Your rules, in your words

Business rules are yours to set in plain language. The worker reads them before every task. No per-app hacks.

Every task on the record

Each task shows what was asked, what was done, where a person stepped in, what it cost, and whether the answer held up.

Your PC, your data

One Windows installer sets up the workers' desktops on your own machine. Share one folder, nothing else.

Secrets stay secret

Passwords are withheld at the source. They never reach the chat, the task record or a recording.
