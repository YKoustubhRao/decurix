# Decurix — YC Winter 2027 founder video

One minute, Koustubh to camera, at a normal speaking pace (no speed-up in the edit). YC asks for a natural video, so learn the bullets and speak from them; the full script is there to rehearse with, not to recite.

## Script (about 160 words, 60–65 seconds)

> Hi, I'm Koustubh, founder of Decurix. I studied computer science at IIT Bombay and worked as a quant developer at QRT.
>
> There, AI wrote most of my code, but I still walked every task through the same steps, every time. Most office work is like that: the intelligence is there, but no one has taught it the job.
>
> Decurix gives an AI worker its own computer. You do a job once on its screen; it learns the steps and the rules, asks about what it couldn't see, and does the next ones itself. It checks its own work against the documents, and when it isn't sure, it asks instead of guessing.
>
> Twenty people at my father's accounting firm use it every day to enter bills into their ERP.
>
> Decurix is the operating system for these workers. You bring the model; the worker and what it learned stay yours. We want anyone who knows a job to teach a worker, run a workforce of them, and sell what they taught to others.

## The same, as bullets to speak from

## Recording

- Phone at eye level, landscape, about an arm's length away; face the light (a window in front of you, not behind).
- Same chair and wall as before is fine. Quiet room.
- Do three or four takes in a row without stopping the camera; the best one is usually the third. Pause a breath between the parts above.
- Send the original file from the phone (Drive or a cable), not through WhatsApp: WhatsApp shrinks the picture to 848x478.
- Aim for 55–65 seconds as spoken. If a take runs long, drop bullet 2's second sentence before speaking faster.

## What changed from the earlier takes, and why

- **It now says what the product does, concretely**, and that 20 people use it every day: the earlier takes said neither, and those are the two facts a partner remembers.
- **Accounting appears as the first deployment, not the product**: the worker is general; the firm is the proof.
- **No jargon**: "semi-structured workflows" and "persistent context" are gone.
- **It ends on the vision**: the OS, your own model, a workforce, and selling what you taught.
